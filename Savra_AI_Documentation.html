1. Executive Summary & Core Identity

1.1 Project Overview: What is Savra AI?

Savra AI is an enterprise-grade Educational Technology (EdTech) platform custom-engineered for Indian K-12 school ecosystems (Grades 1 through 12). Designed to address the immense administrative and cognitive burden borne by educators, Savra AI acts as an intelligent, real-time teaching companion. It empowers educators to generate curriculum-aligned lesson plans, standardized question papers, gamified classroom quizzes, and targeted announcements in seconds rather than hours.

Unlike generic AI conversational tools, Savra AI is deeply grounded in the Indian national curriculum frameworks: the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), the National Curriculum Framework (NCF 2023), Bloomâ€™s Revised Taxonomy, and the 5E Instructional Model (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate). It integrates official CBSE Class 10 and Class 12 blueprint patterns directly into its LLM context window, ensuring that every piece of educational material generated conforms to national board assessment guidelines.

Core Platform Mission: To bridge the gap between ambitious national educational reforms (NEP 2020/NCF 2023) and daily classroom reality by providing teachers with an intuitive, AI-powered pedagogical co-pilot that automates paperwork, personalizes learning, and unlocks deeper student engagement.

2. The Problem Landscape: What Problem Savra AI Solves

The Indian K-12 schooling system is one of the largest and most complex educational environments in the world, serving over 250 million students across public and private institutions. Despite recent transformative reforms, educators and school systems face severe systemic challenges that directly impede educational quality.

2.1 The Crisis of Teacher Administrative Burnout

In standard Indian schools, teachers spend up to 40% to 50% of their working hours on administrative documentation rather than direct classroom instruction or student mentorship:

  • Manual Lesson Planning: Crafting a single multi-period lesson plan compliant with institutional guidelines typically takes 40 to 60 minutes per subject per week. A teacher managing multiple grades spends 8 to 12 hours weekly typing repetitive plan documents.
  • Repetitive Question Paper Drafting: Sourcing questions, balancing mark distributions, ensuring question type diversity, and formatting Word documents consumes hours before every unit test, midterm, and terminal examination.
  • Rote Grading & Record Keeping: Manually scoring objective quizzes and updating mark registers drains teacher energy that could otherwise be directed toward targeted remedial interventions.

2.2 The NEP 2020 & NCF 2023 Implementation Gap

The Government of Indiaâ€™s National Education Policy 2020 (NEP 2020) and the National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF 2023) mandate a dramatic shift from rote memorization toward Competency-Based Learning (CBL), Higher-Order Thinking Skills (HOTS), and experiential pedagogy. However, a massive implementation gap persists:

NEP / NCF Mandate Traditional Classroom Reality Savra AI Algorithmic Solution
Competency-Based Assessments Teachers rely on legacy guidebooks with outdated factual recall questions. Generates application-heavy, case-study, and assertion-reasoning questions directly mapped to learning competencies.
5E Instructional Pedagogy Lesson plans are written as flat summaries of textbook paragraphs without pedagogical staging. Synthesizes Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, and Evaluate steps into every single classroom period automatically.
Bloom's Taxonomy Cognitive Hierarchy Over 80% of teacher-made tests assess solely Level 1 (Remembering) and Level 2 (Understanding). Explicitly balances questions across Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating cognitive tiers.
21st-Century Skills & Real-Life Context Curriculum feels detached from daily life; teachers lack time to research modern contextual applications. Each period plan embeds value education, critical thinking skills, and tangible Indian real-world applications.

2.3 Student Disengagement & Feedback Latency

Traditional homework and paper worksheets suffer from high submission friction and protracted feedback loops. Students receive graded papers days or weeks after submission, by which time the learning moment has passed. Savra AI solves this by introducing instant gamified evaluation: students attempt interactive quizzes on web or mobile, receive immediate score breakdowns with answer justifications, and earn coins that drive competitive leaderboard standings.

2.4 Fragmented EdTech Silos vs. Unified Platform

Prior to Savra AI, schools resorted to a disjointed patchwork of point solutions: one tool for quiz hosting (e.g., Kahoot), another for word processing lesson plans, a third for notice boards (WhatsApp groups), and separate generic AI bots (ChatGPT) that lack curriculum alignment and produce foreign curriculum outputs. Savra AI consolidates the entire teaching and learning lifecycle into a single unified, multi-tenant portal tailored to the Indian educational landscape.

It harnesses Googleâ€™s Gemini 2.5 Flash model configured with low temperatures for deterministic educational outputs.

5.2 Structured Lesson Plan Generation Engine

When a teacher requests a lesson plan for a grade, subject, and chapter, the backend invokes generateLessonPlanPeriods(). The generator produces a strictly structured JSON contract detailing every single teaching period:

Pedagogical Dimension Content Generated per Classroom Period
Concept Clear 2-3 sentence definition of the core scientific or humanities concept to be mastered.
Learning Outcomes Specific, measurable student learning goals mapped to Bloom's taxonomy verbs (e.g., Analyze, Differentiate, Formulate).
Teacher-Learning Process Cohesive 5E instructional sequence synthesizing Engagement, Exploration, Explanation, Elaboration, and Evaluation.
Formative Assessment Rapid, informal comprehension check questions and peer-review tasks conducted within the period.

Student Reflection Metacognitive inquiry prompts encouraging students to self-assess their conceptual understanding.

5.3 CBSE-Compliant Question Paper & Assessment Engine

The assessment generator (generateAssessment()) produces formal examination papers adhering to CBSE sectional architecture:

  • Subject-Specific General Instructions: Automatically embeds official board instructions (e.g., "Section A contains MCQs of 1 mark each", "Use of calculators is strictly prohibited").
  • 9 Standardized Question Typologies:
    1. Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ): 4 balanced options with single correct key.
    2. Short Answer Questions: 2-mark conceptual explanations (2-3 sentences).
    3. Long Answer Questions: 5-mark comprehensive analytical queries.
    4. Case-Study / Source-Based Questions: Contextual passages followed by sub-questions (a, b, c).
    5. Fill in the Blanks: Vocabulary and factual recall statements.
    6. Diagram-Based Questions: Labeling and drawing instructions for biological/physical systems.
    7. Numerical Problems: Multi-step mathematical calculations with formula credit.
    8. Assertion-Reasoning (A-R): Rigorous dual-statement logical evaluations.
    9. Map-Based Questions: Geographical and historical landmark labeling tasks.

5.4 Automated Concise Answer Key Generator

To eliminate teacher evaluation fatigue, generateAnswerKey() immediately parses the newly generated question paper and creates an ultra-concise, plain-text marking scheme. MCQs return the exact option letter and a 1-sentence rationale; numerical problems return final figures and formulas; long-form answers highlight 3 to 5 indispensable scoring criteria.

5.5 Ask Savra Multimodal AI Assistant

The interactive AI assistant (generateAIResponse() & analyzeImage()) dynamically swaps system prompts based on authenticated user roles:

  • Student Mode: Operates as a friendly, patient Socratic tutor. It guides homework without providing copy-paste solutions, gives conceptual hints, and maintains child-safe boundaries.
  • Teacher Mode: Operates as an experienced curriculum specialist. Suggests differentiated classroom strategies, remedial action plans, and analyzes uploaded textbook photos via Gemini Multimodal Vision.

7. User Flows & Operational Journeys

7.1 Teacher Operational Workflow

Step 1: Auth & Guided Onboarding
Teacher signs up using email/password or Google OAuth. Selects institutional school code (e.g., SAVRA001). Selects assigned classes (e.g., Class 10 A) and primary subject faculties. Onboarding status transitions to true.
Step 2: AI-Powered Lesson Planning
Teacher specifies grade, subject, and syllabus chapters. Selects target number of classroom periods and pedagogical objectives. Clicks "Generate with Savra AI". Gemini injects 5E, NEP, NCF, and Bloom's frameworks to create the period table in seconds.
Step 3: In-Browser Customization & Export
Teacher reviews generated periods in the interactive TipTap editor. Customizes learning outcomes, adjusts instructional resources, and saves changes. Exports document as formatted Microsoft Word (.docx) or printable PDF for offline submission.
Step 4: Examination Paper Generation
Teacher navigates to Assessment Creator. Specifies question distribution (e.g., 10 MCQs, 4 Short Answers, 2 Case Studies). Savra AI cross-references official Class 10/12 CBSE sample papers to generate the question paper and produces a concise answer key.
Step 5: Gamified Quiz Publishing
Teacher initiates Quiz Creator. Selects difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) and time limit (e.g., 15 minutes). Gemini generates MCQs with smart distractors. Teacher reviews questions and publishes directly to the designated class section.
Step 6: Class Analytics & Remedial Intervention
Teacher monitors the real-time Analytics Dashboard. Reviews class average scores, individual student completion rates, and the "Top Missed Questions" chart. Uses AI insights to schedule targeted remedial revision periods.

7.2 Student Operational Workflow

Step 1: Enrollment & Dashboard Entry
Student registers with school code and classroom section. Lands on the personalized Student Home showing total coin balance, class rank, pending quizzes, and unread teacher announcements.
Step 2: Gamified Quiz Participation
Student selects an available quiz. Starts interactive timed attempt. Answers MCQs with progress indicators and instant visual feedback. Submits attempt before timer expiration.
Step 3: Instant Score & Points Reward
Backend instantly scores the attempt. Student sees percentage score, correct answers, and explanation tooltips. Earned points are automatically credited to Student.totalPoints.
Step 4: Leaderboard & Socratic Tutoring
Student views class-wide and school-wide Leaderboards. If struggling with a concept, opens "Ask Savra" AI Tutor for step-by-step conceptual hints without direct answer revelation.

7.3 School Administrator Operational Workflow

Institutional Setup & Topology
Admin manages the institutional master record. Verifies classroom sections across Grades 1 to 12. Oversees school code generation and academic year parameters.
Faculty & Roster Supervision
Admin audits the active teacher roster. Assigns subjects and classroom responsibilities. Manages student enrollments, transfers between sections, and roll number allocations.
Academic Output Auditing
Admin accesses institutional analytics: total lesson plans generated, examination papers published, quiz engagement across grades, and faculty adoption metrics.
System Health & Compliance
Ensures curriculum compliance across all school sections in accordance with CBSE circulars and state board regulations.

9. Security, Data Privacy & Reliability Engineering

9.1 Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Security is enforced at every layer of the Savra AI infrastructure:

  • Stateless Cryptographic Tokens: User sessions are managed via digitally signed JWTs containing userId, email, and role (teacher, student, admin). Tokens expire after 7 days, eliminating database session lookup bottlenecks.
  • Granular Authorization Middleware: The authorize(...roles) guard inspects token claims before route handler execution, rejecting unauthorized access with 403 Forbidden responses.
  • Password Hashing: User passwords are encrypted using bcryptjs configured with 12 salt rounds, providing robust defense against rainbow tables and brute-force cracking.
